Why the gaps matter

Four ways data still gets out.

Heavy tools get switched off

When an agent slows laptops, someone finds a reason to remove it. ARGUS has a budget and a watchdog.

Cloud evidence is someone else's

DLP alerts describe your most sensitive data. With ARGUS they stay on your servers.

Watching people isn't protecting data

Screenshots and keystrokes create a privacy problem and still miss the card numbers in a paste.

Logs without content are blind

A SIEM sees a file copied to USB. ARGUS tells it the file held 48 card numbers.
