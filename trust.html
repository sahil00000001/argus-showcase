Tamper protection

Try to switch it off.

You're a local administrator on this laptop. Pick an attack and watch what ARGUS and Windows do.

  • Only the installer, Intune or Group Policy can stop, reconfigure or remove the ARGUS services. Local administrators are refused.
  • If the process is killed, Windows restarts it and ARGUS raises a critical tamper alert.
  • Windows' own logs back this up: service crashes, start-type changes, uninstalls and stop commands are all recorded.
  • File and registry integrity monitoring watches the ARGUS folders and service settings.

Signed policy

Change one character and the laptop refuses it.

One policy file defines the site block list, the site classes, the USB approve list and the modes. It is signed with ECDSA P-256, and laptops accept only signed policy. Below, this page has just signed the policy with a real P-256 key in your browser. Edit it and the signature check runs again.

Policy excerpt

Signing…

  • Tampered, unsigned or older policy is rejected. The last good policy stays in force, and the rejection becomes an alert.
  • One source, two outputs. The same file produces the server lookup lists and the laptop policy, so they never drift.
